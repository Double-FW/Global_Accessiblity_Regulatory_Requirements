# Global Accessibility Regulatory Requirements

This repository is a structured knowledge base and operational guide for global digital accessibility and regulatory compliance management. It brings together jurisdictional overviews, evidence frameworks, reporting guidance, and a proposed agent operating model for organisations that need to plan, evaluate, remediate, report, and govern accessibility obligations.

## Purpose

The repository supports:

- jurisdiction and scope routing across accessibility laws and mandates
- evidence-based compliance planning and evaluation
- process and project management for accessibility work
- operational guidance for human and agent-assisted delivery
- reporting preparation for statements, regulator returns, and procurement evidence

It is designed to help teams understand the relationship between:

- legal or government requirements
- standards and technical methods
- user outcomes and task-based evidence
- policy, governance, and operating controls
- public reporting and procurement documentation

## Repository contents

- `AGENTS.md` — instructions for AI and autonomous agents working in a compliance-management context, including operational boundaries, evidence handling, project governance, and reporting constraints.
- `Global_Website_Accessibility_Regulatory_Overviews.md` — overview of 16 legal/governmental frameworks and reporting formats relevant to website and digital accessibility.
- `Double_FW_Repositories_Regulatory_Delivery_and_Agent_Design.md` — mapping of the public repository ecosystem to compliance workflows, machine/human deliverables, and proposed agent architecture.
- `LICENSE` — MIT license.

## Key themes

### Regulatory and jurisdictional mapping

The repository classifies major accessibility frameworks across regions, including public-sector, equality, procurement, reporting, and consumer-service regimes. The focus is on routing work to the appropriate legal or governance profile rather than assuming one universal rule or standard applies everywhere.

### Evidence-led delivery

The materials distinguish between:

- legal scope and authority
- technical conformance evidence
- functional outcomes and user task success
- policy and process controls
- public statements and service information
- regulator returns and procurement reporting

This separation is intentional: different outputs answer different questions and cannot be treated as interchangeable proof.

### Agent-assisted compliance operations

This repository is explicitly designed to support compliance-oriented agents that can:

- analyse scope and jurisdiction
- identify applicable requirements and source materials
- track evidence, gaps, actions, owners, and review states
- prepare reports, statements, and remediation plans
- execute bounded workflows under proper human authorization

The repository is careful to define limits around legal authority, reporting, procurement claims, regulator submissions, and human sign-off.

### Project and reporting management

The repository supports operational work across:

- accessibility assessment planning
- remediation backlog management
- policy and governance documentation
- public-facing accessibility statements and service information
- regulator, agency, and procurement reporting artifacts
- evidence and change control over time

## How to use this repository

1. Start with `AGENTS.md` if you are building or using an agent for compliance work.
2. Use `Global_Website_Accessibility_Regulatory_Overviews.md` to understand jurisdictional scope, obligation types, and reporting differences.
3. Use `Double_FW_Repositories_Regulatory_Delivery_and_Agent_Design.md` to connect legal duties with recommended methods, source modules, evidence patterns, and delivery architecture.
4. Treat the repository as a structured reference and planning aid, not as a substitute for current legal text, regulator instructions, or binding contract terms.

## Important boundaries

This repository does not claim legal compliance by itself. It provides:

- mapping and structure
- operational guidance
- proposed evidence models and workflows
- implementation recommendations for human and automated support

It does not replace:

- current primary legislation and official regulations
- national or local legal interpretation
- competent human compliance review
- authorised sign-off for public claims or regulator submissions
- verified deployment of tooling or production systems
