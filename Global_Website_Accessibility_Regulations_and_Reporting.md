# Website Accessibility Regulations Around the World

Prepared for Double FW Ltd  
Review date: 4 November 2026

This representative global list distinguishes website-specific rules from broader equality duties and government standards. It covers public-sector and commercial web-based services and their principal reporting requirements.

**VPATs are reporting templates, not regulations.** A completed VPAT produces an Accessibility Conformance Report (ACR); it does not replace a legally required accessibility statement.

## Regulations, coverage and reporting

| Jurisdiction and regulation | Coverage and main website requirement | Reporting or publication requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **EU — [Web Accessibility Directive 2016/2102](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2016/2102/oj)** | Qualifying public-sector websites and apps. Requires perceivable, operable, understandable and robust services; relevant harmonised EN 301 549 provisions support conformity. | Public accessibility statement using the **[EU model](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/en/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A32018D1523)**, feedback mechanism and enforcement information. Member States also conduct monitoring and reporting. |
| **EU — [European Accessibility Act 2019/882](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/882/oj)** | Specified consumer services, including e-commerce, consumer banking and certain transport, communications and e-book services. **It does not cover every commercial website.** | Article 13 and **Annex V** require accessible information explaining the service and how it meets applicable accessibility requirements, in terms and conditions or an equivalent document. National implementation determines enforcement details. |
| **UK — [Public Sector Bodies Accessibility Regulations 2018](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/952)** | Covered public-sector websites and apps. Current government guidance specifies **WCAG 2.2 AA**, subject to applicable exceptions. | Publish and regularly review an accessibility statement using the **[UK model](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/model-accessibility-statement)**, including inaccessible content, alternatives, feedback and enforcement routes. |
| **Great Britain — [Equality Act 2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/15)** | Public and commercial service providers: non-discrimination and anticipatory reasonable adjustments, including access to online services. Northern Ireland uses separate disability legislation. | No general website-specific statement or VPAT requirement under this Act. Evidence should demonstrate effective adjustments and accessible service provision. |
| **USA — [Revised Section 508 Standards](https://www.access-board.gov/ict/)** | ICT developed, procured, maintained or used by federal agencies, including websites. Incorporates **WCAG 2.0 A/AA**, alongside additional ICT requirements. | Procurement commonly requests an **ACR**, usually completed using the Section 508 edition of the VPAT. The solicitation determines the required evidence. |
| **USA — [ADA Title II web accessibility rule](https://www.ada.gov/resources/2024-03-08-web-rule/)** | State and local government websites and apps: **WCAG 2.1 AA**, with specified exceptions. The amended compliance dates are **26 April 2027** for entities with populations of 50,000 or more and **26 April 2028** for smaller entities and special district governments. | No universal VPAT filing requirement under the web rule; organisations need evidence supporting conformity and any claimed exceptions. |
| **USA — [ADA Title III](https://www.ada.gov/law-and-regs/regulations/title-iii-regulations/)** | Businesses that qualify as public accommodations. Requires non-discrimination and effective communication through online services; application can depend on jurisdiction and circumstances. | No general federal Title III website-reporting template or universally prescribed WCAG version. Contracts, settlements and court orders may impose specific evidence requirements. |
| **Canada — [Accessible Canada Regulations](https://www.laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-241/index.html)** | Covered federally regulated public and private organisations. Addresses barriers across ICT and other areas; this is broader than a website technical standard. | Accessibility plans on a **three-year cycle**, progress reports in intervening years, published feedback processes and regulator notification. Sector-specific rules and exemptions matter. |
| **Ontario, Canada — [AODA Integrated Accessibility Standards, section 14](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/110191)** | Designated public bodies and businesses/non-profits with **50+ employees**: covered public websites generally require **WCAG 2.0 AA**, with specified exceptions. | Accessibility compliance reports: generally **every two years for designated public bodies**, **every three years for businesses/non-profits with 20+ employees**. Public bodies also publish annual accessibility-plan status reports. |
| **France — [Law 2005-102, Article 47](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000037388867)** | Public bodies, specified public-interest organisations and companies meeting the **€250 million French turnover threshold**. RGAA supplies the national assessment framework; EAA-derived obligations also apply separately. | Accessibility declaration, published compliance status, a **multi-year accessibility scheme of up to three years**, and annual action plans. |
| **Australia — [Disability Discrimination Act 1992](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A04426/latest)** | Public and commercial digital services. The Human Rights Commission recommends **WCAG 2.2 AA** as a minimum; its technical guidelines are advisory rather than legislation. | No universal website-specific VPAT or public statement requirement under the Act. Assessment and remediation evidence support demonstrating accessible service provision. |
| **New Zealand — [Government Web Accessibility Standard 1.2](https://www.digital.govt.nz/standards-and-guidance/nz-government-web-standards/web-accessibility-standard-1-2)** | A **Cabinet-mandated government standard**, rather than a general commercial law. Requires covered internal and public web pages to meet **WCAG 2.2 AA**, with specified modifications/exceptions. | When notified by the Government Chief Digital Officer: conformance assessment/report, risk assessment and risk-management plan. |
| **Israel — [Service Accessibility Regulations: internet provisions](https://www.gov.il/en/pages/internet_accessibility_regulations)** | Covered public services delivered online, including commercial services. Applies Israeli Standard **5568**, with national modifications and exemptions. | Publish an accessibility declaration and information about accessibility arrangements, alternatives and contact routes. |
| **South Korea — [Disability Discrimination Act, Article 21](https://www.law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1031811973)** | Covered public and private organisations must provide equal access to electronic information. The **[Enforcement Decree, Article 14](https://www.law.go.kr/lsLinkProc.do?chrClsCd=010202&datClsCd=010102&gubun=admRul&joNo=001400006&lsId=2044065&lsNm=%EC%9E%A5%EC%95%A0%EC%9D%B8%EC%B0%A8%EB%B3%84%EA%B8%88%EC%A7%80%EB%B0%8F%EA%B6%8C%EB%A6%AC%EA%B5%AC%EC%A0%9C%EB%93%B1%EC%97%90%EA%B4%80%ED%95%9C%EB%B2%95%EB%A5%A0%EC%8B%9C%ED%96%89%EB%A0%B9&mode=10)** expressly requires accessible websites. | Maintain evidence of accessible provision and required accommodations; a VPAT is not the statutory reporting format. |
| **Brazil — [Brazilian Inclusion Law 13.146/2015, Article 63](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2015/lei/l13146.htm)** | Government websites and companies headquartered or commercially represented in Brazil must provide accessible websites following internationally adopted practices. | The law requires a prominent accessibility symbol. **[Federal-government reporting procedures](https://bibliotecadigital.mj.gov.br/bitstream/1/5123/2/PRI_GM_2017_1.html)** also provide for detailed digital-accessibility reports and improvement work plans; these are not a universal private-sector VPAT duty. |
| **South Africa — [Promotion of Equality and Prevention of Unfair Discrimination Act](https://www.gov.za/ve/documents/promotion-equality-and-prevention-unfair-discrimination-act)** | Broader equality framework relevant to digital access, rather than a dedicated website regulation. Separate **[government website guidance](https://www.gcis.gov.za/guidelines/website/website-accessibility)** recommends **WCAG 2.2 Level A**. | **[Government content guidance](https://www.gcis.gov.za/guidelines/website/maintain-content)** calls for accessibility verification before publication and continuous monitoring; it does not establish a universal commercial VPAT requirement. |

## VPAT and Accessibility Conformance Reports

Choose the [ITI VPAT edition](https://www.itic.org/policy/accessibility/vpat) matching the buyer’s requirements.

| Edition | Intended coverage |
| --- | --- |
| **508** | Revised US Section 508 Standards. |
| **EU** | EN 301 549; useful for European ICT procurement. |
| **WCAG** | WCAG conformance reporting. |
| **INT** | Combined Section 508, EN 301 549 and WCAG coverage. |

A completed **ACR** should identify the tested product/version, evaluation methods, criterion-level support and explanatory findings. It is a supplier’s conformance report, **not independent certification**, and does not by itself satisfy EAA service-information duties or public-sector statement requirements.

See the US government's [ACR/VPAT guidance](https://www.section508.gov/sell/acr-vpat-faq/) and [instructions for creating an ACR](https://www.section508.gov/sell/how-to-create-acr-with-vpat/).

## Scope of this reference

This is a concise planning reference, not an exhaustive inventory of national, state, provincial or sector-specific duties. Applicability, exemptions, national implementation and procurement terms must be checked for the particular organisation and service. Technical standards, statutory publication duties and procurement reporting formats should be recorded separately.
